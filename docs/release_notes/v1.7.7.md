## What changed

### Banner contrast fix (iOS White theme)
- `lib/features/wallpapers/presentation/widgets/pixora_daily_banner.dart` is now **theme-aware** via `context.hud.isDark`.
- **iOS White theme** → "Apple Blue Filled" (concept #01 from user selection 2026-05-06):
  - Solid blue gradient bg `#0A84FF → #0072E0`
  - White icon container with blue rotation icon
  - White Geist 700 17pt title
  - White pill (active/new) with blue text
  - White subtitle at 0.85 alpha
  - Result: high contrast, vibe of iOS primary CTA
- **Black & Gold theme** → keeps the original gold/blue gradient (already had proper contrast on dark surfaces).

### Design archive
- New: `docs/design/pixora_daily_banner_ios_concepts.html` — 5 alternative variants for the iOS theme banner with both states (ACTIVO + NUEVO) shown side-by-side. Concept #01 selected.

### Why this fix
The previous banner used identical color palette regardless of theme. On iOS White (white app background) the light-blue tinted gradient + white text washed out → users couldn't read "Pixora Daily ACTIVO" or interval text. This is a legibility bugfix, allowed under PRODUCTO_TERMINADO pact — existing-feature accessibility patch, not new feature.
