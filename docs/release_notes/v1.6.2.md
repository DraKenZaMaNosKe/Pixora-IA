## What changed

**Stop Story button** (`story_detail_page.dart`)
- Black & Gold: gold deep (unchanged)
- iOS White: iOS red `#FF3B30` matching destructive-action pattern from Settings → Eliminar cuenta

**Home AppBar** (`home_page.dart`)
- When the AppBar sits over a transparent wallpapers tab background and the active theme is iOS White, the dark text on a bright hero image disappeared. Title and search icon now go pure white with a soft black halo shadow for legibility on any image.
- Credits badge: rounded 12px pill with subtle drop shadow on iOS, corner-cut ticket on B&G. Gold border preserved on both for brand continuity.

**Tones list page** (`ringtones_page.dart`)
- AlertDialog (permission), bottom sheet (Set as…), filter chips border, ListTile chevron, error text, section header — all migrated from `Colors.white(70|54|24|12)` literals to theme tokens.

**Bulk white-alpha sweep**
38 occurrences of `Colors.white(54|60|70|24|12|10)` replaced with the matching theme token across:
- `settings_page.dart` (12)
- `aura_player_page.dart` and `sleep_timer_sheet.dart`
- `day_cycle_page.dart`
- `hot_wallpapers_page.dart`
- `stories_page.dart`
- `wallpapers_page.dart`
- `wallpaper_card.dart` (heart unfavorite state, kept on TicketStub variant only)

`const` qualifiers stripped from widgets that became context-dependent. Imports of `hud_tokens.dart` added where missing.

- AI generate wallpaper apply — same bypass as ringtones (lower priority, gated by credits).
- Bulk migration `withOpacity` → `.withValues(alpha:)` (~210 deprecation infos in one pass).
- Catalog merge: `dynamic_catalog.json` and `catalog_index.json` are still two sources of truth. Plan a unified format for v1.7.
