# v1.6.5+41 — ARCANO · Calendario Lunar

Released: 2026-05-02
Track: Closed Testing → Open Testing pending

## ⌬ ARCANO · New section

Lunar wallpapers, mystical tarot, and personalised energy readings.
First entry: **Iah Egyptian Calendar** — cosmic Egyptian goddess
panoramic with the zodiac wheel + your sign highlighted at the centre,
animated moon hero card and personalised reading every day.

- Native panoramic scroll on home (Samsung-friendly 4192×1024 ratio)
- Onboarding asks **name + birth date** — stored ONLY on this device
  (Hive, never uploaded), used to compute zodiac sign, life path,
  Solfeggio personal frequency
- Live zodiac preview in the modal so you can verify the calculated
  sign before saving
- Editable later via the gear icon at the top of ARCANO

## ✦ Wallpaper card cleanup

Wallpapers / Live / 3D listings now show **pure image cards** —
titles and descriptions hidden so you explore by sight without text
imposing a thought.
