Same images, two different rendering tiers. Both map 1:1 to what the Android Kotlin app can produce. Pick the ceiling you want.

Canvas 2D · Particle Physics

JavaScript + Canvas 2D. Real particle system with velocity, deceleration, life cycles — exactly what VolcanoRenderer.kt does natively. 36 embers, 5 torches, ash motes drifting. Same math, rendered in browser.

TIER B

WebGL Fragment Shader · Procedural FX

GLSL fragment shader runs on the GPU. Heat distortion over the volcano (pixel displacement based on a heat map). Volumetric fog rolling across the fortress valley (procedural noise over time). Things that are impossible in CSS and expensive in Canvas 2D — cheap on a shader.
